Week 11 Recap: ctay flips a 13-point underdog spot into a 62.20 blowout, and Turbo Creamer is alone in first

Week 11 Recap

The heaviest bye week of the season did what bye weeks do: the league averaged 105.99 points per team, down from 120.82 in Week 10. The tie at the top is gone. Turbo Creamer (8-3, 1368.74 PF) is alone in first, Jonathan (Taylor's Version) is 7-3-1, and The Mailman slides to 7-4. Here's how it went down.

The games

Turbo Creamer 143.36, The Mailman 81.16 The first-place showdown wasn't close. Yahoo had Jake by 13.29. ctay beat his projection by 42.80, posted the top score of the week, and won by 62.20, the biggest blowout of the week. That's Turbo Creamer's sixth weekly high-score check. Jake came in 32.69 under projection, and 81.16 is The Mailman's lowest score of the season. Week 2's 147.72-91.58 loss has officially been answered.

Slender Father Of 2 126.16, Ted D's Team 76.92 jamie was favored by 16.06 and won by 49.24, beating his projection by 18.50. That's two straight wins and a 6-5 record. Ted D's three-week streak of beating Yahoo's number ends at three: 76.92 is 14.68 under projection and his season low. Ted D's Team is 3-7-1.

Big Load Boy 124.20, Chris Jones's Pecker 89.36 Jacob was favored by 10.95. pmay beat his projection by 32.63 and won by 34.84. Big Load Boy is back over .500 at 6-5 and has the best two-week average in the league (131.68). Jacob has lost two straight and is 6-5.

Jonathan (Taylor's Version) 118.04, ETN Dat Boutte 98.64 Both teams came in under projection (lem0324 by 8.62, Andrew by 12.63), and lem0324 won by 19.40. Andrew has now lost five straight, even though ETN Dat Boutte is third in points for (1318.44) at 4-7.

Boner Nix 105.68, Christian's Team 96.36 The closest game of the week, decided by 9.32. Both teams beat their projections (KevinB by 7.09, Christian by 6.78). KevinB has won three straight and is up to 4-7. Christian has lost four straight and is 3-8.
