Welcome to the iPulp FFL site

The iPulp FFL has a newsroom now

Ten teams. One trophy. Zero chill. Welcome to the official unofficial home of the iPulp FFL, 2026 edition.

Here's what's on the site:

  • Standings: the full table with W-L-T, points for and against, and streaks. The dashed line marks the playoff cut (top 6).
  • Results: every week, every score, plus the top score and biggest blowout of each week.
  • Power Rankings: our own formula mixing record, points for and recent form. Yes, it will offend you. No, it's not personal. It's math.
  • Teams: a page per squad with the roster by slot, a season game log and recent moves.
  • News: recaps, previews and whatever else the league desk feels like writing.
  • Transactions: every add, drop and trade, with a search box for the nosy.
  • League Info: half PPR, 4-point passing TDs, $100 FAB, keepers, and a trade deadline of November 28, 2026.

All numbers come straight from Yahoo Fantasy. If a stat's on this site, it came from the league page, not from somebody's imagination.

Got a hot take, a recap you want to write or a grievance to air? Send it to the commish. Now go set your lineups.
