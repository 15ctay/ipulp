Week 6 Preview: byes bite, the Mailman defends first, and ETN keeps hunting

Week 6 Preview

The first sole first-place team of the year has a target on its back, and Week 6 byes (CIN / DET / MIA / MIN) are about to scramble a few lineups. Projections below are from Yahoo as of the latest scrape (standings updated Mon Oct 12, ~11:52pm PDT). Scores are still 0–0 everywhere.

Turbo Creamer (proj 93.75) vs Ted D's Team (proj 104.67)

ctay is riding a three-game win streak and just cashed the Week 5 high-score prize, but Yahoo likes Ted D by about 11. Turbo's projection is the second-lowest on the board this week — bye week and matchup noise in one package. Ted D (2-3) needs this one to stay in the thick of the 2-3 pack.

Slender Father Of 2 (proj 103.81) vs Big Load Boy (proj 108.03)

Two teams that both won last week, separated by about 4 projected points. jamie is coming off 158.46; pmay just took down Jonathan (Taylor's Version). Big Load Boy is 3-2 and slightly favored. Watch the DET/CIN bye tax on both benches.

Jonathan (Taylor's Version) (proj 79.23) vs The Mailman (proj 102.43)

The standings leader (4-1) draws the former power-rankings king — and Yahoo has lem0324 all the way down at 79.23, the lowest projection of the week (hello, DET bye). Jake gets a chance to pad the sole lead. Upset fuel if Jonathan's non-bye pieces cook.

Boner Nix (proj 112.42) vs ETN Dat Boutte (proj 127.27)

Andrew is the new #1 in power rankings, leads the league in PF (705.06), and is on a three-game heater. KevinB is 1-4 and projected for a respectable 112.42 — enough to win some weeks, probably not this one if ETN hits its number.

Christian's Team (proj 107.27) vs Chris Jones's Pecker (proj 105.39)

Toss-up on paper (1.88 apart). Christian is 2-3 with the second-most PF in the league; Jacob is 2-3 with three straight losses. Someone's skid ends here. Probably.

Desk picks

Ted D's Team, Big Load Boy, The Mailman, ETN Dat Boutte, and Christian's Team in the coin-flip. Don't take it personally. Picks are made to be broken — especially in a bye week.
