The Guerendo Game: How KevinB's Two-Year Crush on Isaac Guerendo Cost Him a Ring

Every fantasy manager has a guy. A late-round crush they keep around long after the evidence says to move on. For KevinB, that guy was Isaac Guerendo, and the relationship ran two seasons, three team names and one championship game.

Editor's note: all love. Every number below is from Yahoo: KevinB's weekly lineups and the league's matchup history.

The courtship

KevinB has been team 5 the whole way: Michael Penis Jr. (2024), Bech Shots (2025) and Boner Nix (2026).

  • 2024 draft: Guerendo, Round 16, pick 7. A last-round flier. Fine. Everybody has one.
  • 2025 draft: Guerendo again, Round 14, pick 6. He moved up two rounds. Nobody else in the league took him in either draft, and nobody else ever added him. It was a one-man fan club.

Across those two seasons, Guerendo sat on KevinB's roster for 21 weeks. He started 3 of them, for 47.20 points total. The other 18 weeks he lived on the bench, scoring things like 0.00, 1.90, -2.00, 2.20 and 1.20. That's not a stash. That's a long-distance relationship.

Week 6, 2024: the first missed date

The first time the bench decision really mattered was 2024 Week 6. Guerendo scored 9.90 on the bench while Ty Johnson started at RB and put up 3.40. Michael Penis Jr. lost to Maker Baefield 86.72-103.20.

In fairness to KevinB, swapping them is only +6.50, and he lost by 16.48. It wouldn't have flipped. But it set the pattern: when Guerendo scored, he was on the bench. Week 8? 17.70 on the bench. Week 13? 8.10 on the bench.

Then KevinB finally committed

In December, KevinB finally put a ring on it, so to speak:

  • Week 14: Guerendo at FLEX, 25.80. A 145.46-126.92 win. The best bench option, Jameson Williams, had 10.50. Great call.
  • Week 15 (playoffs): Guerendo at RB, 9.50, in a 157.64-75.34 rout of Commander Cream. (That's ctay's team, so Guerendo got to help embarrass Corey. Thank you, Isaac.)
  • Week 16 (semifinal): Guerendo on the bench, 0.00, and a 150.88-98.96 win over Commander James. Michael Penis Jr. was in the final.

Week 17, 2024: the Guerendo Game

The 2024 championship: Michael Penis Jr. vs Commander Louis (lem0324), Week 17.

KevinB started Isaac Guerendo at FLEX. Guerendo scored 11.90.

On the bench: Jameson Williams, 22.00.

Final score: Commander Louis 133.48, Michael Penis Jr. 131.08. KevinB lost the title by 2.40.

Do the math. Williams over Guerendo is +10.10. That's 141.18 to 133.48. That's a win, that's a title, and that's a banner on the Hall of Fame with KevinB's name on it. Instead, lem0324 got his second ring, and KevinB got the runner-up finish that's still the only top-two finish in his ten seasons.

It's worse than that. That one lineup slot reaches all the way up to the Mount Rushmore. lem0324 sits at #1 with a success score of 46.58. Swap that title (10 points for a title, 5 for a runner-up) and lem0324 drops to 41.58, which would be 4th, behind jamie and Jake. KevinB jumps from 25.07 to 30.07. Isaac Guerendo's 11.90 decided who's carved first on the mountain.

The breakup

KevinB drafted him again in 2025 anyway. Love is blind. It's also, apparently, on the PUP list.

Guerendo opened 2025 on PUP: 0.00, 0.00, 0.00, 0.00 in Weeks 1-4, all on the bench. Then, on Oct 1, 2025, at 9:37 am, it ended: KevinB dropped Guerendo to waivers and added the Rams DEF.

Two drafts, 21 weeks, a championship start, and he got dumped for a team defense. Not even a player. A logo.

And Guerendo hasn't been on an iPulp roster since. Nobody claimed him in 2025, nobody drafted him in 2026, and he's not in a single 2026 transaction.

The kicker: seven weeks after the breakup, KevinB dropped 191.78 on Ted D's Team in 2025 Week 11, the highest weekly score in league history. Some people need to get over somebody to reach their potential.
