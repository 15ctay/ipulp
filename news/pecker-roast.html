Shrinkage: Chris Jones's Pecker Trades Away Ja'Marr Chase and Goes Limp

Shrinkage: Chris Jones's Pecker Trades Away Ja'Marr Chase and Goes Limp

Jacob gave the 3-1 Mailman a superstar for three guys and a prayer, and one of them is already on his bench. A roast of last year's runner-up (allegedly) and this year's worst trade.

Editor's note: This is a roast. It's all love, and it's strictly about fantasy football. Every 2026 number comes straight from Yahoo. Last season's finish is league lore, and Jacob can take it up in the group chat.

The Trade

Saturday, October 10, 7:31 pm. While the rest of the league was setting lineups, Jacob was busy ruining his.

Chris Jones's Pecker receives: Rome Odunze, Quinshon Judkins, Michael Wilson. The Mailman receives: RJ Harvey and Ja'Marr Chase.

Read it again. He traded Ja'Marr Chase. A true, certified, plug-him-in-and-go-to-brunch WR1. In return he got a three-for-two bundle. That's not a trade, that's a clearance-rack combo meal. And in the same minute he dropped Dontayvion Wicks, just to make room for more mediocrity.

And who'd he give the superstar to? The Mailman, already 3-1, already second in the standings, already sitting on 518.36 PF. Jacob looked at a team tied for the best record in the league and said, "You know what you need? Ja'Marr Chase." The Mailman didn't just take the deal, he got a delivery. Jacob signed for the package himself.

The best part: as of Saturday night, Rome Odunze is on Jacob's bench. He traded Chase for a guy he isn't even starting. Michael Wilson and Quinshon Judkins made the lineup, and Odunze is riding the pine next to a Joe Mixon he picked up on Oct 4 who's listed NA. You can't make this up, and we didn't. It's all on Yahoo.

The Fluke

League lore says Chris Jones's Pecker finished second last season. We don't have last year's numbers here, and honestly, that's for the best, because nobody wants to relive it. Let's call it what it was: an absolute fluke. A fantasy blip. A one-season growth spurt that has since shrunk back to normal size.

Because the 2026 version? It's showing everybody what this franchise really is.

By the Numbers

  • Record: 2-2. Rank: 6th, hanging onto the last playoff spot by the hem of its shorts.
  • Streak: L-2, and trending down.
  • Points for: 424.46, 7th in the league. Slender Father Of 2 is 1-3 and has still outscored him.
  • Points against: 456.16.
  • The decline: 157.62, then 105.26, then 89.42, then 72.16. Every week smaller than the last. The Pecker started the season huge and has been shrinking every single week. Doctors have a word for this. So do fantasy managers.
  • Weeks 2–4 combined: 266.84. That's less than double his Week 1 score.

Exhibit A: The Game Log

Week 1: W, 157.62 to 87.54 (+70.08) vs. Boner Nix. The top score of the week. Jacob strutted around like he'd figured out fantasy football. Enjoy the memory, because it's the high point of the season.

Week 2: W, 105.26 to 99.58 (+5.68) vs. Big Load Boy. Scraped by on a 5.68-point margin. The first sign that the Pecker was losing steam.

Week 3: L, 89.42 to 92.06 (−2.64) vs. The Mailman. A 2.64-point loss to The Mailman. So Jacob lost to him, then sent him Ja'Marr Chase. Most people hold a grudge after losing a close one. Jacob mailed the guy a gift basket.

Week 4: L, 72.16 to 176.98 (−104.82) vs. Jonathan (Taylor's Version). The biggest blowout of the season, the lowest score of Week 4, and the season-high score for lem0324. Jacob lost by more points than his entire team scored. Chris Jones's Pecker didn't show up, it shriveled up.

Week 5 (live): 0.00 to 54.00 vs. Slender Father Of 2. Down 54 before his guys have touched the field, with a 118.10 projection against Slender Father Of 2's 145.61, the highest in the league this week. jamie is 1-3, and even he's laughing at this one.

The Waiver Wire Rap Sheet

The trade wasn't a one-off. It's a pattern.

  • Sep 30, 1:14 am: added the Giants D on a $0 waiver claim. Sep 30, 4:39 am: dropped the Giants D for the Raiders. Three hours and 25 minutes. That defense didn't even get to unpack.
  • Sep 30: dropped Romeo Doubs for Keenan Allen. A week later ETN Dat Boutte paid $20 to get Doubs. Then Jacob dropped Keenan Allen too, on Oct 7, for Keon Coleman ($10).
  • Oct 4: dropped Michael Pittman Jr. for Joe Mixon, who's now sitting on the bench marked NA.

He's not managing a roster. He's running a catch-and-release program.

The Prescription

  1. Start the guy you traded Chase for. Odunze on the bench makes the trade look even dumber.
  2. Stop the catch-and-release. Three hours with the Giants D. A week with Keenan Allen. Pick someone and commit to them.
  3. Quit losing to The Mailman, then arming him. You lost to him by 2.64 in Week 3, then gave him a superstar. Pick a side.
  4. Grow it back. 157.62, 105.26, 89.42, 72.16. Reverse the shrinkage. Science says it's possible. Your lineup says otherwise.
  5. Stop living off last year. A second-place finish (allegedly) is a fluke, not a résumé.

The trade deadline is November 28. There's still time to make another trade, but please, for the love of the league, consult literally anyone first.
