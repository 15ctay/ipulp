Limp, Last and Leg Day-Less: Boner Nix Can't Get It Up Off the Floor

Limp, Last and Leg Day-Less: Boner Nix Can't Get It Up Off the Floor

Fewest points in the league. Two defenses on one roster. A $1 bench D bought with Khalil Shakir. KevinB is 1-3, dead last, and trying to climb out of the basement on the littlest calves in fantasy football.

Editor's note: This is a roast among friends. Every number comes straight from Yahoo. The calves bit is a long-running league inside joke, and KevinB knows exactly what he did to earn it.

Rock Bottom

There are ten teams in the iPulp FFL. Boner Nix is 10th.

1-3. 376.56 points for, the fewest in the league. Even Ted D's Team, which is second-lowest at 384.58, is looking down at him. KevinB has managed to finish last in both the standings and the scoring, which takes real commitment.

The team is named after his own quarterback, Bo Nix, which is fitting, because four weeks in, this roster is as limp as the name suggests. Big talk on draft night. Couldn't perform on Sundays.

And the foundation of the whole operation? It's like Kevin's calves: small, underdeveloped, and visibly struggling to hold up anything heavier than a participation trophy. This team skipped leg day. Every leg day.

By the Numbers

  • Record: 1-3. Rank: 10th of 10. Streak: L-1.
  • Points for: 376.56, last in the league. That's 94.14 a week. The league leader, Jonathan (Taylor's Version), has 555.22, which is 178.66 more. That's about two whole Boner Nix weeks.
  • Points against: 505.88, the second-most in the league. Okay, opponents have been tough. But when you score the fewest points, nobody's crying over your schedule.
  • Sub-90 weeks: three of four (87.54, 86.62, 76.46). The one exception (125.94) came against Big Load Boy, and we'll get to that miracle.
  • FAB spent on a backup defense: $1. More on that crime below.

Game Log of Shame

Week 1: L, 87.54 to 157.62 (−70.08) vs. Chris Jones's Pecker. The biggest blowout of Week 1. Opening day, and Boner Nix lost by 70.08 to a team that has since scored 72.16 in a single week. KevinB walked onto the field on those little calves and fell over.

Week 2: L, 86.62 to 139.80 (−53.18) vs. Christian's Team. The lowest score of Week 2. Lost by 53.18. Two weeks, two blowouts, and a combined 174.16 points. Christian's Team scored 139.80 by itself.

Week 3: W, 125.94 to 102.58 (+23.36) vs. Big Load Boy. Suddenly, a pulse. Boner Nix rose to the occasion with 125.94, by far his best score of the year, and took down Big Load Boy by 23.36. For one glorious Sunday, the boner was not limp. The calves held. Briefly.

Week 4: L, 76.46 to 105.88 (−29.42) vs. Ted D's Team. And then it all deflated. 76.46, the second-lowest score of Week 4, against a Ted D's Team that has the second-fewest points in the league. KevinB lost to the only team that's even close to his level of bad. A total collapse. No legs. Literally.

Roster and Transaction Roast

Sep 30: Bears D on a $1 waiver, dropped Khalil Shakir. KevinB cut a wide receiver to add a second defense. Then, on Oct 7, Big Load Boy paid $15 for that same Shakir. So Kevin cut a guy someone else valued at $15 to make room for a defense he valued at $1. Fifteen times the value, out the door. The Bears D is still on the bench today, behind the Jaguars. Two defenses, one starting spot. That's not depth, that's hoarding.

Oct 7: Will Shipley on a $19 waiver, dropped Tank Bigsby. Nineteen dollars to swap one Eagles RB for another. Shipley is now on his bench, right next to Saquon Barkley, listed as Out. KevinB built an Eagles backfield museum and none of the exhibits are playing.

The bench: Josh Jacobs, Matthew Golden, Carnell Tate (Q), Fernando Mendoza, Kenyon Sadiq, Antonio Williams, Will Shipley, Saquon Barkley (O) and the Bears D. Big names, zero points. They're not a bench, they're a waiting room.

The starters: Bo Nix at QB (the namesake, the brand, the problem), Cam Skattebo, Jeremiyah Love, Parker Washington, Jaylen Waddle, Tucker Kraft, Drake London, Bhayshul Tuten and the Jaguars D. Talent on paper. Calves in practice.

Week 5: A Chance to Stand Up?

Here's the twist. As of Saturday night, Boner Nix trails The Mailman just 0.00 to 1.90, and Yahoo projects Kevin to win, 112.75 to 108.40. Yes, the last-place team is the projected favorite over the 3-1 Mailman, who just traded for Ja'Marr Chase.

So this is it, Kevin. Your big chance to rise up. Just don't put too much weight on it. We've all seen what those calves can carry.

The Prescription

  1. Do a leg day. For the lineup, and honestly, for yourself.
  2. Pick one defense. The Jaguars and the Bears are both on your roster. Fantasy football allows one starter. You're paying a roster spot for a decoration.
  3. Stop dropping players other people want. Khalil Shakir was $15 to Big Load Boy and $0 to you.
  4. Score 100. You've done it exactly once (125.94). Do it again before Halloween.
  5. Get it up off the floor. 376.56 PF, dead last. Nowhere to go but up, if those calves can make the climb.

The trade deadline is November 28. Plenty of time to rebuild. Bring a step stool.
