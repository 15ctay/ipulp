Week 6 Recap: Turbo Creamer repeats the $30 high score as the Mailman hits 5-1

Week 6 Recap

The CIN / DET / MIA / MIN byes took a bite out of everybody. The league averaged just 103.93 points per team, the lowest weekly average of the season so far. The Mailman is still alone at the top at 5-1, and two teams behind him have now won four straight. Here's how it went down.

The games
